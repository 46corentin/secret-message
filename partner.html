A behavioural platform for responsible disposal in shared spaces.

For wipe, tissue & hygiene brands

Responsible disposal should feel natural.

Millions of wipes and disposable hygiene items are used every day. Many are still flushed improperly — not because people are careless, but because bathrooms rarely make the right behaviour feel discreet, simple or inviting.

Secret Message turns proper disposal into a small, thoughtful ritual.

The problem

The hidden cost of convenience.

Across Europe, wastewater operators spend millions of euros every year addressing sewer blockages and fatbergs, with wet wipes recognised as a major contributor.

In the UK alone, water companies estimate that sewer blockages cost approximately £200 million every year, with wet wipes recognised as a major contributor. Learn more about fighting fatbergs →

The solution

A platform, not only a product.

Secret Message is a discreet paper-based disposal system and a communication platform that brands can use to support better disposal habits in hotels, transport, workplaces and other shared spaces.
